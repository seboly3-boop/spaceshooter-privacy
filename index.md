PRIVACY POLICY

Game: Space Shooter
Effective date: November 20, 2026
Developer: 3Boly
Contact: seboly3@gmail.com

1. SUMMARY
Space Shooter is an offline game. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. Everything the game remembers stays on your device.

2. INFORMATION WE DO NOT COLLECT
The game does not ask you to create an account or sign in. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, device identifiers or advertising ID. The game contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools.

3. INFORMATION STORED ON YOUR DEVICE
To let you continue playing, the game saves the following in its private app storage on your device only:
- Game progress: coins, best score, unlocked weapons and upgrades, player level and XP, lifetime statistics (kills, bosses defeated, runs, combo records), missions, achievements and daily rewards.
- Settings: sound on/off, music and effects volume, battery saver.
This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data in your Android settings. The game opts out of Android's automatic app-data backup.

4. INTERNET AND PERMISSIONS
The game does not need an internet connection and does not request internet access or any special Android permissions such as location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage.

5. THIRD PARTIES
We do not share data with third parties because we do not collect any. If you download the game from a store such as Google Play, the store collects its own information under its own privacy policy, and we do not receive it.

6. CHILDREN
The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. YOUR CHOICES
Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can erase all game data at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

8. SECURITY
Game data stays on your device and is protected by Android's app sandbox. Keep your device updated and protected with a screen lock.

9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
If a future version of the game changes how data is handled (for example by adding online features or ads), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the new effective date will be shown here.

10. CONTACT
Questions about this policy: seboly3@gmail.com
